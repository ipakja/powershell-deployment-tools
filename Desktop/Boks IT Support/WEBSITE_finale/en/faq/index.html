FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers

Is BIT an in-house IT department?

No. BIT takes structured ownership of clearly scoped user, access and workplace requests, documents them and follows them through. Scope is agreed in writing first.

Which companies is this for?

Smaller companies with about 5–50 employees, mostly on Windows and Microsoft 365, without a dedicated IT role — Zurich and remote across Switzerland.

Can every IT problem be covered?

No. BIT takes on clearly scoped user, access and workplace tasks within a written scope. Complex infrastructure, vendor-dependent or specialist topics are not automatically included and are reviewed before work starts.

Does BIT only work with hotels?

No. Hotels and front desk are one experience example. The offer is aimed at smaller Swiss companies without in-house IT.

Who makes technical changes?

It depends on the case and the written agreement. Where a task sits outside my own scope, I check first whether a suitable external specialist can be involved.

How are access changes approved?

Permission and access changes only happen after documented approval and within the written scope.

Are there fixed packages or prices?

There are guideline rates (working time CHF 120 per hour). The IT basics check is a fixed-price entry from CHF 190 with a written finding and priorities. Most requests are handled remotely. If an on-site appointment is required, it is agreed in advance and listed separately in the quote — Zurich area, with no hidden travel flat fee. Prices become binding only after written review of scope.
