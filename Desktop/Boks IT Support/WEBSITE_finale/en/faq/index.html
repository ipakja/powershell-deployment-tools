FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers

Which companies is BIT for?

Smaller companies with about 5–50 employees without in-house IT, mostly on Windows and Microsoft 365 — Zurich and remote across Switzerland.

Does BIT replace an in-house IT department?

BIT is for smaller companies that do not need a full internal IT role, but want one central contact for defined user, access and workplace services.

Can every IT problem be covered?

No. BIT focuses on user, access, Microsoft 365 and standardised workplace services. For more complex specialist topics, BIT first checks whether they can be handled or coordinated within the service.

Does BIT only work with hotels?

No. Hotels and front desk are part of the operational background. The offer is aimed across industries at smaller Swiss companies without in-house IT.

Who makes technical changes?

BIT owns the commissioned service and remains your contact. Standardised tasks are delivered inside the BIT service. When specialist expertise is required, BIT coordinates suitable specialist work within the agreed engagement.

How are access changes approved?

Access and permission changes only happen after approval by a person authorised by the company.

Are there fixed packages or prices?

The IT basics check starts at CHF 190. Further services are offered by scope as a fixed price, recurring service, or — for additional special effort — after prior approval. Before work starts, you receive the agreed scope and price. Remote is the default; Zurich-area on-site visits are agreed separately.

How quickly does BIT respond?

As a rule you receive a first reply within two working days with an assessment of feasibility and the next step.

Must our IT already be documented?

No. Especially when users, access or ownership are not fully documented, the IT basics check can serve as a starting point.

Does your need fit BIT?

Have your service need reviewed without obligation. You will receive an assessment of whether and how a collaboration could work.
