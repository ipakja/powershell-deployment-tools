Privacy

This website provides an inquiry form for needs assessment. When you submit the form, the details you enter are processed so BIT can assess your needs and contact you. Alternatively, you can reach BIT by email, phone or WhatsApp.

Categories of data collected

  • Company
  • First and last name
  • Business email address
  • Number of employees
  • Requested service area
  • Short problem description
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Location / canton (optional)
  • Industry (optional)
  • Number of workstations (optional)
  • Microsoft 365 status and internal/external IT situation (optional)
  • Preferred start timing (optional)
  • Technical transmission metadata (e.g. timestamp, request ID; possibly IP country via the hosting provider)

Purpose of processing

Needs assessment: reviewing whether and on what basis collaboration is possible, and contacting you with an initial assessment.

Legal basis

Consent. You give it via the mandatory checkbox on the inquiry form before submitting.

Retention

Inquiries are stored for a maximum of twelve months and then deleted, unless statutory retention duties or legitimate grounds require longer storage.

Recipients and processors

Details from the inquiry form are delivered to admin@boksitsupport.ch and stored for needs assessment. In addition, an operator-configured short messaging notification (for example Telegram or Slack) may be triggered so the inquiry is seen promptly. Optionally, an email notification may be sent via an email delivery service (Resend). Website hosting: Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare and, if used, Resend or the chosen messaging service may operate servers outside Switzerland.

Possible processing abroad

Depending on the delivery path (messaging notification if enabled, email delivery service if enabled) and hosting (Cloudflare), processing or storage may take place outside Switzerland or the EEA. WhatsApp may process contact, device and communication metadata outside Switzerland if you use that channel.

Your rights

You may request access to, rectification or deletion of your personal data and withdraw consent for the future. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Privacy contact

For privacy requests: admin@boksitsupport.ch. Do not send passwords, recovery codes or specially protected personal data through the form or messaging apps.

There is no invasive tracking and no advertising measurement. When you visit the site, the hosting provider may process technically necessary server logs.
