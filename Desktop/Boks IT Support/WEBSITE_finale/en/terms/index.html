1. Scope

1.1 These Terms and Conditions apply to all services that BIT – Boks IT Support, owned by Stefan Bogdanovic (hereinafter «BIT»), provides to customers.

1.2 Services are offered exclusively to companies, self-employed professionals and organisations. Contracts with private individuals for private purposes are not concluded.

1.3 Diverging or supplementary terms of the customer apply only if BIT agrees to them in writing. This also applies where BIT provides a service without reservation despite knowledge of such terms.

1.4 The version of these Terms in force at the time the contract is concluded is decisive.

2. Contracting party and formation of contract

2.1 The sole contracting party of the customer is BIT.

2.2 Inquiries via the inquiry form, email, phone or messaging apps do not constitute an engagement. Submitting an inquiry creates neither a contract nor an obligation for BIT to process the request.

2.3 A contract is formed when BIT issues a quote or order confirmation in text form and the customer accepts it in text form. Email and messaging messages also qualify as text form.

2.4 The quote or order confirmation records at least: the subject of the services, the scope boundaries, the remuneration and — where agreed — the timeframe.

3. Services and service limits

3.1 BIT provides services in organising and handling clearly scoped tasks relating to user accounts, access, Microsoft 365-related standard requests and everyday workplace topics. The concrete scope follows from the respective agreement.

3.2 BIT owes careful performance of the mandate, not a specific result. Unless otherwise agreed, the Swiss Code of Obligations rules on mandates (Art. 394 et seq. CO) apply.

3.3 In particular, the following are not part of the services:

  • on-call cover outside agreed times or round-the-clock operations;
  • unlimited flat-rate support without a defined scope;
  • work outside the written confirmed engagement scope;
  • services for which BIT lacks the required permissions, access or expertise;
  • assuming overall responsibility for the customer’s IT infrastructure.

3.4 Response and handling times are only committed where expressly agreed in writing. Without such agreement, work is handled as reasonably practicable and within a reasonable period.

3.5 Work is generally delivered remotely. Where an on-site engagement is required, it is agreed in advance and listed separately in the quote.

4. Involvement of third parties

4.1 BIT may involve suitable third parties to fulfil the engagement. In that case BIT remains the customer’s contracting party.

4.2 Where involving third parties creates additional costs not already agreed, BIT informs the customer before placing the order; the customer’s consent in text form is required.

4.3 If no suitable specialist is available for a task, BIT informs the customer. There is then no entitlement to performance of that task.

5. Customer cooperation

5.1 The customer ensures that BIT receives the information, contact persons and access needed to fulfil the engagement in good time.

5.2 The customer designates a person authorised to approve changes to user accounts, permissions and access. BIT carries out or initiates security-relevant changes only after documented approval by that person.

5.3 The customer is responsible for backing up their data. Before work that may cause data loss, a current and verified backup must be in place. BIT does not owe data backup unless expressly agreed.

5.4 The customer ensures that any access provided is lawfully granted and that sharing data with BIT is permitted.

5.5 Delays caused by missing or late cooperation are not attributable to BIT. Additional effort arising from this may be charged on a time basis.

5.6 Passwords, recovery codes and specially protected personal data must not be submitted via the inquiry form, email or messaging apps.

6. Fees, expenses and payment terms

6.1 The remuneration agreed in the quote or order confirmation applies. Unless otherwise agreed, billing is on a time basis at the then-current hourly rate.

6.2 Figures on the website are guideline rates. Only the written confirmed terms are binding.

6.3 Started time units are billed according to the increment set in the quote.

6.4 Travel time and expenses for on-site work are charged only where agreed in advance.

6.5 Invoices are payable within 30 days of the invoice date without deduction. After that period the customer is in default without further reminder; default interest of 5% per year under Art. 104 para. 1 CO is owed.

6.6 BIT may require advance payment or instalments, especially for first engagements.

6.7 All amounts are in Swiss francs. While BIT is not subject to VAT, no VAT is shown; a corresponding note appears on the invoice.

7. Deadlines and delays

7.1 Stated deadlines are non-binding unless expressly agreed in writing as binding.

7.2 If an agreed deadline cannot be met, BIT informs without delay and aligns on next steps.

8. Confidentiality

8.1 BIT treats all information obtained in the course of the collaboration as confidential and uses it solely to fulfil the engagement.

8.2 The confidentiality obligation continues after the collaboration ends.

8.3 Where third parties are involved, they are bound to confidentiality to the same extent.

8.4 BIT may describe the collaboration in anonymised form as a reference only if the customer has expressly consented in text form.

9. Data protection

9.1 BIT processes personal data in accordance with the Swiss Data Protection Act. Details are set out in the privacy notice on boksitsupport.ch.

9.2 Where BIT processes personal data on the customer’s behalf, this is done solely within the customer’s instructions and the agreed purpose. An additional data-processing agreement can be concluded on request.

10.2 Liability for slight negligence is excluded to the extent permitted by law.

10.3 To the extent permitted by law, liability is limited to the amount of the remuneration agreed for the relevant engagement.

10.4 To the extent permitted by law, liability for indirect and consequential damage is excluded, including lost profit, business interruption, data loss and third-party claims.

10.6 BIT is not liable for third-party products, services or outages, including software and cloud providers, network operators and hardware manufacturers.

10.7 Complaints must be notified in text form without delay after becoming aware and no later than 10 days after delivery of the service.

12.3 The right of either party to revoke or terminate at any time under Art. 404 CO remains reserved. Services already performed must be paid for in any event.

12.4 On ending the collaboration, existing documentation is handed over on request in a customary format.
