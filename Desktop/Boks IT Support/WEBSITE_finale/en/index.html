Current standard service is not intended for…

BIT takes on clearly scoped tasks with a written objective. Service frame and responsibility are set before work starts.

  • no 24/7 incident cover
  • no open-ended retainer work
  • no work outside the confirmed service frame

Typical situations

  • A new employee starts and needs access
  • A former employee still has active accounts
  • A role change needs adjusted rights and mailboxes
  • A standardised Microsoft 365 or workplace request

How it works

Customer → BIT → specialist partner inside the BIT service if needed → quality check → documented close-out. The customer relationship is not handed off.
