How I proceed

Five consistent touchpoints structure each agreed engagement.

01

Preparation

I clarify the objective, written scope, required materials and the secure access method. I also record what is outside the engagement.

02

Arriving at work

I start remotely or on site as agreed. I first verify the working environment and confirm the goal for that session.

03

Daily work

I complete the tasks agreed in writing, such as PMS, folio or channel cases, Microsoft 365 or device work. I record changes and findings within the agreed scope.

04

Special tasks

I take on one-off exceptions only under a separate mini-scope agreed in writing. This does not create open-ended or unlimited support.

05

Leaving

I hand over findings, open items and the next step, then close the engagement cleanly. I never send confidential credentials through messaging apps or request them there.
