About me

I am Stefan Bogdanovic and run BIT as a sole proprietorship in Zurich.

I support small and medium-sized businesses with clearly scoped IT and digital tasks. For the Swiss B2B market, I conduct structured pilot mandates. I first assess the starting point, dependencies and limits, then document the agreed results. If an engagement requires additional expertise, I state this before work starts and involve external specialists only by agreement. I remain the contractual counterparty.

I work independently and am not an official representative of any PMS or software vendor.
