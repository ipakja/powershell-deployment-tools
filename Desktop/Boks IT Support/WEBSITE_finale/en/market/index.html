Swiss Market Development

From document check to a Swiss market pilot.

I assess with you whether and how a clearly defined offer can be positioned and tested with selected Swiss businesses.

Swiss Market Development

Document check

Your offer, capacity profile or company text in Swiss business German. CHF 190 per document, five working days, one revision round.

Second step

The six-week pilot

The pilot is a scoped validation mandate with documented outputs.

  • Kick-off and written scope for the offer, target segment and assumptions
  • Review of positioning, value proposition, pricing logic and existing material
  • A reasoned list of suitable target businesses and contacts
  • Agreed initial outreach in BIT's name; representation only under an explicit written mandate
  • Weekly record of activity, responses and open questions
  • Final report with observations and a sober recommendation for next steps

Price

Price

  • Document check: CHF 190 per document
  • Swiss pilot fixed price: CHF 4'900
  • Pilot payment: 50% on commissioning and 50% after the final report

Optional after the pilot

A follow-on mandate is agreed only with mutual interest, a written scope and separate remuneration. I involve external specialists only after prior agreement. I remain the contractual counterparty.

What I do not do

What I do not do

The pilot does not use invented performance evidence and cannot create market demand. It provides a documented basis for a decision; a reasoned negative result is also useful.

  • No commitment of customers, revenue, contracts or market success
  • No legal, tax, customs or certification advice
  • No authority to represent, conclude contracts or collect funds without an explicit written mandate
  • No travel costs, translations or third-party services in the fixed price
