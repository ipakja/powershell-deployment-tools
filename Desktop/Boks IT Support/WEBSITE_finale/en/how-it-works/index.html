Process

How it works

Customer → BIT → specialist partner inside the BIT service if needed → quality check → documented close-out. BIT remains your central contact throughout the engagement.

Six steps

  • 1Submit an inquiry
  • 2Review need and feasibility
  • 3Confirm scope and price
  • 4Deliver the service and coordinate specialist work if needed
  • 5Review the outcome
  • 6Document the close-out

What to expect

After the inquiry

Reply usually within two working days with an assessment of feasibility and the next step.

When does an engagement start?

Only once scope and price are confirmed. Submitting the form alone does not create a contract.

Specialist work when needed

If specialist expertise is required, BIT coordinates that work inside the agreed service. BIT remains your contact.

Approvals

Access and permission changes only happen after approval by a person authorised by the company.

Extra effort

Additional special effort arises only after prior approval and is stated up front.

Documented close-out

At the end you receive a clear outcome with completed items and remaining open points.

Does your need fit BIT?

Have your service need reviewed without obligation. You will receive an assessment of whether and how a collaboration could work.
