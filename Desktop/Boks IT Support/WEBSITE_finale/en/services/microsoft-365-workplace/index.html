For standardised workplace and Microsoft 365 cases.

When sign-in, Outlook or Teams stalls at a workstation, work time is lost immediately. BIT narrows whether the account, device or service is affected and handles clearly scoped standard cases. Result first: less disruption and one contact through to documented close-out. Remote is the default; Zurich-area on-site visits only by agreement and listed separately.

Typical situations

  • Sign-in or MFA blocks the start of work.
  • Outlook or Teams fails at a workstation.
  • Recurring standard workplace disruptions cost time.
  • A workstation blocks the working day through recurring sign-in failures.

Process

  • 1Capture the issue and impact
  • 2Check whether it fits the standard scope
  • 3Implement the standard case or coordinate necessary specialist work
  • 4Document the outcome and open points

Required details

  • Affected workstation or user
  • Error message or observed behaviour
  • Since when and how often it occurs

Boundaries

Vendor-dependent or deep infrastructure topics are not automatically included. A specialist is involved only by agreement. Without enough detail (error picture, timing, affected workstation) the case cannot be scoped reliably. Critical infrastructure or production environments are outside this entry point.
