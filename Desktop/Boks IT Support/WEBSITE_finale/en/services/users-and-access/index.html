For joiners, leavers and role changes.

User and access topics often arise under time pressure: a Monday joiner, a leaver without a clear account list, a role change with new rights. BIT captures the request in a structured way, clarifies approvals and records the agreed scope. Result first: a productive start or closed access — documented and traceable. Most cases are handled remotely; Zurich-area on-site visits only when needed and listed separately. Approvals stay with the company.

Typical situations

  • A new employee should be productive on Monday.
  • A departure is coming and active access must be closed.
  • A role change needs adjusted rights and mailboxes.
  • Roles and shared mailboxes must be approved in a traceable way and later revoked cleanly.

Process

  • 1Capture the need and required details
  • 2Clarify approvals and ownership
  • 3Implement approved changes or coordinate necessary specialist work.
  • 4Document the close-out

Required details

  • Name and start or leave date
  • Required systems and roles
  • Who may grant approvals

Boundaries

Specialist technical cases outside the agreed scope are reviewed before work starts. No 24/7 cover and no unlimited scope. BIT does not replace an internal IT department and does not take unlimited retainer work. Before accepting an engagement, BIT reviews whether the requested service is feasible.
