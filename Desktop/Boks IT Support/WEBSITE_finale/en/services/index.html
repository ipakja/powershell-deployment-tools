Services

Three clear entry points

For companies with about 5–50 employees in Zurich and remote across Switzerland. BIT reviews and handles clearly defined standard cases within a written scope. Each service starts with a written scope. Remote is the default; Zurich-area on-site appointments are agreed in advance and listed separately in the quote — with no hidden travel flat fee.
