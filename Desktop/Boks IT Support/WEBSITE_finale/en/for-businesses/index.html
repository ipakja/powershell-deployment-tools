Audience

Who this is for

A good fit for

  • about 5-50 employees
  • mostly Windows and Microsoft 365
  • no dedicated IT role
  • recurring joiners and leavers
  • a need for documented handling

Not the right frame for

  • private individuals
  • large enterprises
  • critical infrastructure
  • specialised production IT
  • unlimited 24/7 support
  • requirements without a clear scope

BIT’s role

Intake, structuring, coordination, communication, documentation, follow-up and billing.

Specialist topics and third-party costs are discussed in advance; see the section «One contractual partner, one contact person» on the home page.

Scope, responsibilities and billing are set in writing before work begins. I remain the contractual counterparty.

Worked examples

All examples are sample flows, not documented client cases.

Sample: new employee starts

Capture required access, written approval, coordinate setup, documented close-out.

Sample: leaver without clarity

List existing accounts and rights, coordinate disablement, document close-out and confirmation.

Sample: Outlook does not work at a workstation

Structured intake, clarify ownership, follow progress, record the outcome.

Sample: unclear vendor ownership

Collect details, contact the responsible party, track status and document the close-out.
