Offer

Services for small businesses without an in-house IT team

BIT provides structured user, access and digital workplace services for Swiss businesses with approximately 5–50 employees.

Typical services

  • employee onboarding and offboarding
  • user accounts, roles and access changes
  • Microsoft 365 and workplace requests
  • coordination with specialised delivery partners
  • documented completion and follow-up

Best suited for businesses that…

  • have approximately 5–50 employees
  • do not employ an in-house IT role
  • use Windows and Microsoft 365
  • have recurring user and access changes

Outside the current standard service

  • guaranteed 24/7 support
  • full IT infrastructure management
  • complex server and network operations
  • advanced cybersecurity projects
  • critical or highly regulated environments

Additional specialist services may be coordinated separately where a suitable delivery partner is available.

BIT’s role

Intake, structuring, coordination, communication, documentation, follow-up and billing.

Specialist topics and third-party costs are discussed in advance; see the section «One contractual partner, one contact person» on the home page.

Scope, responsibilities and billing are set in writing before work begins. I remain the contractual counterparty.

Worked examples

All examples are sample flows, not documented client cases.

Sample: new employee starts

Capture required access, written approval, coordinate setup, documented close-out.

Sample: leaver without clarity

List existing accounts and rights, coordinate disablement, document close-out and confirmation.

Sample: Outlook does not work at a workstation

Structured intake, clarify ownership, follow progress, record the outcome.

Sample: unclear vendor ownership

Collect details, contact the responsible party, track status and document the close-out.
