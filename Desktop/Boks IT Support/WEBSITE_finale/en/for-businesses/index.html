Offer

User, access and workplace services for small businesses

BIT offers clearly defined services for Swiss businesses with approximately 5–50 employees without an in-house IT role.

Typical services

  • employee onboarding and offboarding
  • user accounts, roles and access changes
  • Microsoft 365 and workplace services
  • coordination of specialist work when required
  • documentation and follow-up

Best suited for businesses that…

  • have approximately 5–50 employees
  • do not employ an in-house IT role
  • use Windows and Microsoft 365
  • have recurring user and access changes

Outside the current standard service

  • guaranteed 24/7 support
  • full IT infrastructure management
  • complex server and network operations
  • advanced cybersecurity projects
  • critical or highly regulated environments

Before accepting an engagement, BIT reviews whether the requested service is feasible. Additional specialist services may be coordinated inside the BIT service when needed.

BIT’s role

Intake, service coordination, quality control, communication, documentation and close-out.

Before accepting an engagement, BIT reviews feasibility. If specialist expertise is required, it is coordinated within the agreed BIT service. BIT remains your central contact.

Scope, responsibilities and billing are set before work begins. The contractual partner is BIT (sole proprietorship Stefan Bogdanovic).
